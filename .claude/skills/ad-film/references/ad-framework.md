# Ad framework (checked 2026-09-27)

## Craft rules
- One proposition per ad. If a line does not serve it, cut it.
- Show, then say: the demo shot proves the claim; VO names the benefit, not the spec list.
- Product appears early (by 5s) and last (packshot). Brand/slogan in the final 3s.
- Sound: a recurring sound logo at the packshot makes the channel/brand recognisable across ads.
- Keep one visual system per brand: colours, font, caption style, packshot layout.

## Legal and platform rules (Korea)
- Economic interest must be disclosed clearly at the start (affiliate commission, paid production posted on our account).
  Source: 공정위 추천·보증 심사지침 개정 (2024-12-01) https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156660604
- No fabricated reviews, rankings, discounts, before/after or usage results; no efficacy claims without evidence.
  (표시·광고의 공정화에 관한 법률 — deceptive/unsubstantiated advertising) https://www.law.go.kr/법령/표시ㆍ광고의공정화에관한법률
- Do not present an affiliate video as the brand's official ad: no brand logos/slogans used as if we were the brand, no brand name in our slogan. Trademark and misleading-origin risk.
- AI-generated realistic footage: use each platform's AI label (Instagram AI info, YouTube altered/synthetic, TikTok AIGC).
  YouTube: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Music and SFX: only synthesized (sound.py) or licensed audio; never lift audio from reference videos.

## Client work (selling ads to sellers)
- Get product photos and claim approval from the client in writing.
- The client owns final approval; deliver master + cutdowns + clean version.
